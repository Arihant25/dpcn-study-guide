A plan for the three days

There are 15 proofs here. That's five a day, which is doable.

  1. Monday. Learn the formulas for all four parts. Then the Part 1 proofs (2-D stability and limit cycles), because the other parts reuse its Jacobian method.
  2. Tuesday. Parts 2 and 3 (maps and Lorenz). Then write Monday's proofs again from memory.
  3. Wednesday. Part 4 (synchronisation). Then do the five past questions above with the page closed, and check each against its proof.
  4. Thursday morning. One round of flashcards, and read the formulas once more.

For every proof, learn the shaded one-line idea first, then the steps. Cover the page and write it out, because writing is what makes it stick. The 2026 mid-sem gave 0 for a formula with no derivation, so always write the steps, even when you remember the answer.
