DPCN Mid-sem Guide

This guide assumes you know nothing about the course. It covers only what is on the mid-sem syllabus. Every topic starts in plain words with a small example, every symbol is explained when it first appears, and every step of the maths is explained in words. The formulas to memorise are collected at the end of each section.

The earlier, longer guide for Quiz 1 is still available as the Quiz 1 guide.
