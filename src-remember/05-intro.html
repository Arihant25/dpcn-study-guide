How to use this page

You need both. The formulas are what you start from and what you box at the end. The proofs are where the marks are, and the 2026 mid-sem gave 0 for a formula written without its derivation.

  1. Learn the formulas by heart. They're short lists, one per page.
  2. Then read the two questions and the recipe. Most proofs follow one of them, so you have far fewer separate things to learn than it looks.
  3. Learn each proof in two layers. First its one-line idea (the shaded line). Then the steps, each with its reason after "∵". If you remember the idea, you can usually rebuild the steps.
  4. Cover the proof and write it on paper. Then check it against the page. Writing it out is what makes it stick, not rereading.
  5. Use the flashcards. "Facts" drills the formulas, "Proofs" drills the derivations. Cards you miss come back later in the round.

A few proofs a day for a week beats all of them the night before. (Annoying, but true.)
