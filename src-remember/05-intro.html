How to use this page

Reading the guides again and again won't make them stick. Testing yourself will. So here's the plan.

  1. Read the formula lists below once. Don't try to memorise them yet.
  2. Read the two questions and the recipe. They tie most of the formulas together, so you have far fewer separate things to remember than it looks.
  3. Go to the flashcards. Say the answer out loud (or write it) before you tap "Show answer". Cards you miss come back later in the round.
  4. When a card keeps beating you, follow its link to the full explanation in the guides.

Ten minutes of flashcards a day beats two hours of rereading the night before. (Annoying, but true.)
