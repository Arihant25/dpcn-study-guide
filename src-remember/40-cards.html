Page 1What does the handshaking lemma say?›

\(\sum_i k_i = 2L\). Every link has two ends, so it adds 2 to the total degree. That's why \(\langle k\rangle = 2L/N\).

Quiz 1 guide §3

Page 1What does \([A^n]_{ij}\) count?›

The number of walks of length \(n\) from \(i\) to \(j\). So \([A^2]_{ii} = k_i\), and the number of triangles is \(\frac16\operatorname{tr}(A^3)\).

Quiz 1 guide §4

Page 1Why divide \(\operatorname{tr}(A^3)\) by 6 to count triangles?›

Each triangle shows up as a closed walk from each of its 3 corners, in 2 directions. \(3\times2 = 6\).

Quiz 1 guide §4

Page 1When does a connected graph have an Euler circuit? An Euler trail?›

Circuit if every node has even degree. Trail (start and end differ) if exactly 2 nodes have odd degree, and it runs between them. More than 2 odd nodes means neither.

Quiz 1 guide §5

Page 1Formula for the clustering coefficient of node \(i\)?›

\(C_i = \dfrac{2L_i}{k_i(k_i-1)}\), the links among its neighbours divided by the most there could be. A complete graph has \(C = 1\). A tree has \(C = 0\).

Quiz 1 guide §7

Page 1Name the four centralities and what each measures.›

Degree (how many links), closeness (\(1/\sum d\), how near everyone is), betweenness (how many shortest paths pass through you), eigenvector (\(A\mathbf x = \lambda_{\max}\mathbf x\), linked to important nodes).

Quiz 1 guide §8

Page 1How many links does a tree on \(N\) nodes have? A complete graph?›

A tree has \(N - 1\). A complete graph has \(N(N-1)/2\).

Quiz 1 guide §9

Page 1Write one step of Euler's method. Which derivative formula is more accurate, forward or central?›

\(y_{n+1} = y_n + h\,f(y_n)\). Central is more accurate, with error \(O(h^2)\) against \(O(h)\) for forward.

Quiz 1 guide §10

Page 2For \(P(k) = Ck^{-\gamma}\), what is \(C\)?›

\(C = (\gamma-1)k_{\min}^{\gamma-1}\), from making the probabilities add up to 1.

Mid-sem guide §1

Page 2For a power law, when are ⟨k⟩ and ⟨k²⟩ finite? Where do real networks sit?›

⟨k⟩ needs \(\gamma>2\). ⟨k²⟩ needs \(\gamma>3\). Real networks usually have \(2<\gamma<3\), so the average is finite but ⟨k²⟩ blows up.

Mid-sem guide §1

Page 2Why use log binning for a power law?›

With equal-width bins, the tail bins hold 0 or 1 node, so the plot is noisy and zeros can't go on a log axis. Log bins grow by a fixed factor, so the wide tail bins collect enough nodes. Divide each count by \(N\) times the bin width.

Mid-sem guide §2

Page 2Average path length of a star, an open chain and a ring?›

Star \(2(N-1)/N\) (about 2). Chain \((N+1)/3\). Ring about \(N/4\). Ring where each node has degree \(k\): about \(N/(2k)\).

Mid-sem guide §3

Page 2In an ER graph, how are \(p\), ⟨k⟩ and \(N\) related? What is the degree distribution?›

\(\langle k\rangle = p(N-1)\). Binomial, which becomes Poisson \(e^{-\langle k\rangle}\langle k\rangle^k/k!\) for large \(N\) and small \(p\). Its variance equals ⟨k⟩.

Mid-sem guide §4

Page 2Equation for the giant component, and when does it exist?›

\(S = 1 - e^{-\langle k\rangle S}\). It exists only when \(\langle k\rangle>1\). Just above that, \(S\approx2(\langle k\rangle-1)\), so the exponent \(\beta = 1\).

Mid-sem guide §5

Page 2When is an ER graph fully connected?›

When \(\langle k\rangle > \ln N\).

Mid-sem guide §5

Page 2ER graph: average path length and clustering? How do real networks compare?›

\(\langle l\rangle\approx\ln N/\ln\langle k\rangle\) and \(C = p = \langle k\rangle/N\). Real networks also have short paths, but much higher clustering.

Mid-sem guide §6

Page 2Is ⟨knn⟩ equal to ⟨k⟩?›

No. \(\langle k_{nn}\rangle = \langle k^2\rangle/\langle k\rangle \ge \langle k\rangle\), because popular nodes show up on many friend lists. For ER it's \(\langle k\rangle + 1\).

Mid-sem guide §7

Page 2How do you test whether a fixed point of \(\dot x = f(x)\) is stable?›

Stable if \(f'(x^*)<0\), unstable if \(f'(x^*)>0\).

Mid-sem guide §8

Page 2Give the normal form of each of the four 1-D bifurcations.›

Saddle-node \(r - x^2\) (two points meet and vanish). Transcritical \(rx - x^2\) (two points swap stability). Supercritical pitchfork \(rx - x^3\) (one point splits into three). Subcritical pitchfork \(rx + x^3\).

Mid-sem guide §9

Page 2What is hysteresis?›

With two stable states (stable, unstable, stable), the system jumps at one value of the parameter going up and at a different value coming down. Turning the knob back doesn't undo the jump. The jumps happen at folds, where \(f = 0\) and \(f' = 0\).

Mid-sem guide §10

Page 2SIS: endemic level, and when does the disease die out?›

\(I^* = 1 - \gamma/\beta\). It dies out when \(\beta<\gamma\), because then \(f'(0) = \beta - \gamma < 0\).

Mid-sem guide §11

Page 2Define the Laplacian. What do its zero eigenvalues tell you?›

\(L = D - A\). The number of zero eigenvalues equals the number of connected components. All eigenvalues are \(\ge 0\).

Mid-sem guide §12

Page 2On a network, when does SIS spread?›

When \(\beta/\gamma > 1/\kappa_1\), where \(\kappa_1\) is the largest eigenvalue of \(A\). Early on the infection grows like \(e^{(\beta\kappa_1-\gamma)t}\mathbf v_1\).

Mid-sem guide §13

Page 3For \(\dot{\mathbf x} = A\mathbf x\), what equation gives the eigenvalues?›

\(\lambda^2 - \tau\lambda + \Delta = 0\), with \(\tau = a + d\) (trace) and \(\Delta = ad - bc\) (determinant).

After mid-sem guide §1

Page 3Classify a 2-D fixed point from \(\tau\) and \(\Delta\).›

\(\Delta<0\): saddle. Otherwise the sign of \(\tau\) gives stability (negative is stable), and \(\tau^2 > 4\Delta\) gives a node, \(\tau^2<4\Delta\) a spiral. \(\tau = 0\) with \(\Delta>0\) is a center.

After mid-sem guide §2

Page 3What is the Jacobian, and how does it decide stability?›

\(J_{ij} = \partial f_i/\partial x_j\), evaluated at the fixed point. If every eigenvalue has negative real part, it's stable.

After mid-sem guide §5

Page 3Rabbits vs sheep: what kind of point is coexistence, and what does it mean?›

\((1,1)\) is a saddle (\(\lambda = -1\pm\sqrt2\)). The stable states are "rabbits only" and "sheep only", so one species always wins. This is competitive exclusion.

After mid-sem guide §6

Page 3Predator–prey (Lotka–Volterra): fixed points and their types?›

\((0,0)\) is a saddle. \((d/c,\ a/b)\) is a center with \(\lambda = \pm i\sqrt{ad}\), so the populations cycle around it.

After mid-sem guide §7

Page 3Write \(\dot z = (\mu + i\omega - |z|^2)z\) in polar form. What happens at \(\mu = 0\)?›

\(\dot r = \mu r - r^3\), \(\dot\theta = \omega\). For \(\mu>0\) a stable limit cycle of radius \(\sqrt\mu\) appears and the origin turns unstable. That's a supercritical Hopf bifurcation.

After mid-sem guide §9

Page 3What does the Poincaré–Bendixson theorem say?›

In 2-D, if paths are trapped in a region that has no fixed point, there must be a closed orbit in it. It only works in 2-D, which is why chaos needs at least 3 dimensions.

After mid-sem guide §10

Page 3How does Euler's method with \(\Delta t = 1\) turn a logistic equation into the logistic map?›

\(\dot x = x(K-1) - Kx^2\) becomes \(x_{n+1} = x_n + (K-1)x_n - Kx_n^2 = Kx_n(1-x_n)\).

Page 3Logistic map: where does the period double, and where does chaos start?›

Period 2 at \(r = 3\), period 4 at 3.449, period 8 at 3.544. Chaos from about 3.57.

After mid-sem guide §13

Page 3Lorenz system: the fixed points, and when is the origin stable?›

\((0,0,0)\), stable iff \(\rho<1\). For \(\rho>1\) also \(\left(\pm\sqrt{\beta(\rho-1)},\ \pm\sqrt{\beta(\rho-1)},\ \rho-1\right)\). Chaos above about \(\rho = 24.74\) (with the usual \(\sigma = 10\), \(\beta = 8/3\)).

After mid-sem guide §14

Page 3Two coupled oscillators: when do they lock, and at what frequency?›

They lock when \(|\omega_2 - \omega_1| \le k_1 + k_2\). The common frequency is \(\Omega = \frac{k_2\omega_1 + k_1\omega_2}{k_1+k_2}\), the plain average if \(k_1 = k_2\).

After mid-sem guide §15

Page 3What is the Kuramoto order parameter, and what is \(K_c\)?›

\(re^{i\psi} = \frac1N\sum_j e^{i\theta_j}\). \(r\) near 0 means out of sync, near 1 means in sync. Sync starts at \(K_c = 2/(\pi g(0))\), which is \(2\gamma\) for a Lorentzian.

After mid-sem guide §16

Page 3State May's stability criterion.›

A large random system with \(S\) species, connectance \(C\), interaction spread \(\sigma\) and self-damping \(d\) is stable if \(\sqrt{SC\sigma^2} < d\). Bigger or more connected systems are less stable.

After mid-sem guide §17

Page 3Double pendulum with equal masses: the two normal-mode frequencies?›

\(\omega = \sqrt{g/L}\,\sqrt{2\pm\sqrt2}\).
