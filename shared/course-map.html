The derivations to learn for Quiz 2 and the end-sem

Past papers ask for proofs and derivations. These are the ones the 2024 papers asked, or that the slides work through, roughly in order of how likely they are. Learn to write each one from memory.

  1. Solve the SIS model, endemic level, when the disease dies out · Mid-sem §11
  2. Fixed points, stability and the type of bifurcation for a 1-D system · Mid-sem §9
  3. Fixed points and Jacobian of a 2-D or 3-D system, and classification (rabbits vs sheep, predator–prey, Lorenz) · After §6, §14
  4. Stability of a map \(x_{n+1} = f(x_n)\); the logistic map · After §12
  5. Power law: the constant \(C\), ⟨k⟩, ⟨k²⟩ and when they diverge · Mid-sem §1
  6. Giant component \(S = 1 - e^{-\langle k\rangle S}\) and the threshold ⟨k⟩ = 1 · Mid-sem §5
  7. Binomial to Poisson · Mid-sem §4
  8. Limit cycle in polar form, Hopf bifurcation · After §9
  9. Two coupled oscillators: locking condition and common frequency · After §15
  10. SIS on a network and the threshold \(1/\kappa_1\) · Mid-sem §13
  11. Number of triangles \(= \frac16\operatorname{tr}(A^3)\), and \([A^n]_{ij}\) counts walks · Quiz 1 §4
  12. Handshaking lemma and Euler's theorem on Eulerian paths · Quiz 1 §3, §5
  13. Closeness and betweenness on a path or star; eigenvector centrality by the power method · Quiz 1 §8
  14. Average path length of star, chain and ring · Mid-sem §3
  15. ⟨k_nn⟩ = ⟨k²⟩/⟨k⟩ and the friendship paradox · Mid-sem §7
